# Crater-ion (C-ION)

**Safety-Critical Autonomy Software for Precision Lunar Landing**

---

Lunar landers fail during final descent not because of structural problems — but because their software wasn't built to handle the real world. Dust blinds cameras. Terrain doesn't match the map. Sensors contradict each other. And traditional guidance systems have no safe response.

**Crater-ion closes that gap.**

We build a modular autonomy software stack that connects four things no one has connected before: what the spacecraft *sees*, how *confident* it is in what it sees, which sensor to *trust* when inputs conflict, and a mathematical *guarantee* that the resulting action is safe — even when the AI upstream is wrong.

The result is a landing system that doesn't just perform well in simulation. It fails safely in the real world.

---

## What We Sell

A licensable software layer for lander primes, GNC integrators, and mission operators — not a lander, not hardware. Our stack plugs into existing mission architectures as a validated autonomy module, reducing landing risk and providing the safety-assurance story that mission certification requires.

**Phase 1** — Simulation and digital-twin validation toolkit  
**Phase 2** — Onboard inference package, licensed per mission  
**Phase 3** — Co-development with lander primes and GNC supply chains  
**Phase 4** — Standalone safety-certification tooling, extensible to terrestrial robotics

---

## Why Now

Commercial lunar missions are accelerating. 40+ missions are planned between 2024 and 2030 across NASA, ESA, and New Space operators. Two of the last four commercial landers crashed on descent. Mission operators need autonomy they can certify — not just demonstrate in ideal conditions.

No European player currently offers a closed-loop, certifiable autonomy stack for precision landing. We're building it.
